## Why this is a request and not a licence term

This is stated plainly so no one is misled.

CriGent's interface is built with PyQt6, which is licensed under **GPL-3.0-only**.
Section 10 of the GPL says a distributor "may not impose any further
restrictions on the exercise of the rights granted or affirmed" by that licence.
Adding an enforceable "Islamic use only" condition on top of GPL-licensed code
would therefore violate the GPL, and any such clause would be legally void.

So the author does not pretend to restrict your rights. What is written above is
a covenant of conscience — an appeal to the person using the software, not a
term a court would enforce. It is offered in the belief that a sincere request,
honestly labelled, is worth more than a clause that cannot stand.

If you accept it, use it well. If you do not, your legal rights under the GPL are
unaffected, and the matter is between you and Allah.

## A note on the agent features

CriGent can execute commands on the computer it runs on. It asks for your
approval before each one, and the "Auto-run" switch removes that approval step
entirely. Whoever enables it carries responsibility for what follows. Please
weigh that seriously — for your own machine and for anyone else's.
